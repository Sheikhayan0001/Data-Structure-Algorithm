#======================== Conversions ===================

5. Infix to Postfix Conversion
Example:
Infix:   A+B*C
Postfix: ABC*+


Rules:
1. Operand → Add directly to output.
2. ( → Push into stack.
3. ) → Pop until (.
4. Operator → Pop higher or equal precedence operators, then push current operator.
